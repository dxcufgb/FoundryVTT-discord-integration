Servers and campaigns

Where each Discord server gets which messages, who is pinged and who may plan sessions, and the channels of its campaigns: the same settings as /channel, /gm-role and /campaign edit in Discord. A message type without its own channel goes to the default channel. Saving warns when the bot lacks a permission in the chosen channel.

The bot is not in any Discord server yet.

Session summaries

When a world goes back to setup, the bot posts the session summaries the Foundry module dxcufgbs-session-summary wrote for it, to the channel of every campaign bound to that world (a campaign without a channel gets none). Each summary is posted once per channel. Without the module nothing is posted and the bot works as before.

    Post now

    Posts what is due for one world right away, as if it had just stopped (summaries that were already posted are skipped).
